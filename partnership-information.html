Hosting Partnership Information

About BettaCraft SMP

BettaCraft SMP is a private, whitelisted Minecraft server for content creators. We are a long running SMP, which means we spend more than a year in the same world before moving on to a new one. Each season brings new ideas, new base themes and stories waiting to be told. We build our own large bases and bring everything together into one integrated world.

The server has different districts such as the Shopping District where we sell items for diamonds, and a Minigames District for games.

Right now BettaCraft SMP has a small creator team, with AJ Zoomer being the main active content creator. We are preparing to invite new creators soon, and we need better server infrastructure to support more players and future content.

At the moment the server is hosted on local hardware and is not able to run 24 hours a day. As the project grows and more creators join, we need a hosting partner who can provide stable uptime and performance moving forward.

Season 5 is planned for next year, but the creator expansion is happening soon. This is why we are looking for a hosting partner ahead of time.

What We Are Looking For

We are currently seeking a hosting provider who can supply:

  • 8 to 12 GB RAM Minecraft server (Java Edition)
  • Reliable 24/7 uptime
  • Support for plugins, datapacks and custom resource packs
  • DDoS protection
  • Room to scale as the SMP grows

This server will host the main BettaCraft SMP world including creator bases, districts, and more.
